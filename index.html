How this forecast works

Weather forecasts: Open-Meteo. Wave forecasts: Open-Meteo Marine. Tide predictions: NOAA CO-OPS where a mapped station is available. All displayed times are America/Chicago. Wave heights are requested in feet. Species-specific experimental scores combine wind, gusts, rain, pressure trend, regional wave height, and hourly predicted tide movement; moon phase and illumination are approximated astronomically and are not scored until validated. Pressure trend is an approximate change across the available forecast hours, not a measured barometer reading.

Data refreshes automatically every six hours while the page is open, and when returning to a stale page. The manual Refresh button remains available. Compare the daily average with the peak hour when planning your trip. The model has not been validated against catch records, and scores may differ from other forecasts. Missing hourly inputs leave the corresponding scores unavailable.
