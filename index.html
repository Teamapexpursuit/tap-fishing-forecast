How this forecast works

Weather forecasts: Open-Meteo. Wave forecasts: Open-Meteo Marine. Tide predictions: NOAA CO-OPS where a mapped station is available. All displayed times are America/Chicago. Species-specific experimental scores combine wind, gusts, rain, pressure trend, wave height, and hourly predicted tide movement; moon phase is approximated astronomically but is intentionally not scored until validated.

Data refreshes automatically every six hours while the page is open, and when returning to a stale page. The manual Refresh button remains available. This is a read-only prototype. No registration, tracking, or connection to Shark Watch, Catch Watch, Sharkotic, or your live website.
